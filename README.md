# WheatFHB Atlas｜小麦赤霉病抗性区间图谱

WheatFHB Atlas is a public-benefit, evidence-layered map of published wheat Fusarium head blight resistance records. It supports chromosome-by-chromosome exploration, coordinate and gene searches, and batch SNP overlap analysis.

小麦赤霉病抗性区间图谱是一个公益性在线工具，用于分层整理、展示和查询已有文献报道的小麦赤霉病抗性记录。网站支持逐条染色体浏览、基因/坐标/区间查询，以及批量 SNP 与有证据抗病区域的重叠分析。

- Laboratory / 课题组：Zhang Cuijun Lab（张翠军课题组）

Version v0.3.0 separates causal/functional genes from unvalidated candidates. Seventeen exact RefSeq gene models reported as putative candidates by Zheng et al. 2021 are displayed on an off-by-default candidate track and excluded from resistance-region overlap. Eighteen secondary curated literature leads are also excluded pending full-text coordinate verification. Original-study and later coordinate/projection sources are stored separately for all 624 original-QTL records.

v0.3.0 将已验证/功能基因与未验证候选基因拆分显示。Zheng et al. 2021 报告的 17 个精确 RefSeq 基因模型现归入默认关闭的候选轨道，不计入抗病区域命中；18 条二次整理的文献线索在完成全文坐标复核前同样不计入命中。624 条原始 QTL 的原始研究来源与后续坐标/投影来源已分别记录。
